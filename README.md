# Hola — I build free, practical web tools and guides

I'm sarasithjoy. I run small, free websites that answer everyday questions: how to track a parcel, how to check who called from an unknown number, and how to turn exam scores into a result you can actually use. No sign-ups, no paywalls — just clear, useful pages.

## My websites

### 🇲🇽 Paqueteriarastreo — Guías de rastreo y facturación en México
Spanish-language guides for tracking parcels and invoices with Mexican couriers like Paquetexpress and Estafeta.
- [paqueteriarastreo.com](https://paqueteriarastreo.com/)
- [Guía de facturación de Paquetexpress](https://mixwellnew.github.io/guia-rastrear-paquetes-mexico/)

### 🇮🇹 Paccotraccia — Guide per tracciare i pacchi in Italia
Italian-language guides for tracking Poste Italiane parcels, registered mail and international shipments, step by step.
- [paccotraccia.it.com](https://paccotraccia.it.com/)
- [Come tracciare un pacco Poste Italiane](https://mixwellnew.github.io/guida-tracciare-pacchi-italia/)

### 🇹🇷 LGS Puan Hesaplama — Ücretsiz LGS puan hesaplama aracı
A free Turkish tool that converts LGS net scores into points, including the free points the state adds.
- [lgspuan-hesaplama.tr](https://lgspuan-hesaplama.tr/)
- [LGS'de devlet kaç puan veriyor?](https://mixwellnew.github.io/lgs-puan-hesaplama-rehberi/)

### 🇵🇱 KtoDzwonil24 — Sprawdź, kto dzwonił z nieznanego numeru
A free Polish reverse phone-lookup tool: check an unknown number before you call back.
- [ktodzwonil24.pl](https://ktodzwonil24.pl/)
- [Sprawdź numer telefonu](https://mixwellnew.github.io/kto-dzwonil-poradnik/)

All four sites are free to use. Suggestions and corrections are welcome — open an issue on any of the guide repositories.
